📖 ShapeMorph 3D Guide & Maths Lab

1. What is a 2D Net?

A 2D net is a flat two-dimensional pattern formed by unfolding the faces of a 3D solid along its edges. When cut and folded along the crease lines, it joins seamlessly into the closed 3D solid without any overlapping faces.

2. Plans & Elevations (Orthographic Projections)

Architects and engineers visualize 3D structures from 3 perpendicular viewpoints:

  • Plan View: What you see looking straight down from directly above (bird's-eye view).
  • Front Elevation: What you see standing directly in front of the shape.
  • Side Elevation: What you see looking from the left or right side profile.

3. Surface Area vs. Volume

  • Total Surface Area: The combined 2D area of all outward-facing surfaces ($cm^2$). Simply calculate the area of each face in the flat net and sum them up!
  • Volume: The total 3D space enclosed inside the closed solid ($cm^3$). For uniform prisms: $\text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}$. For pyramids and cones: $\frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}$.

4. Euler's Characteristic
