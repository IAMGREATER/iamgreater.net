THE 30-YEAR CIRCLE

From Turbo Pascal to Agentic Vibe Coding

From MS-DOS text-mode databases to autonomous AI swarms: the journey behind IAMGREATER.

1996 – 1998 💾

The Spark: Turbo Pascal & CD Database

Learned to code in Borland Turbo Pascal 7.0 on MS-DOS. Built a custom relational database to catalogue an extensive physical CD collection—manually wrangling pointers, records, binary file I/O, and blue-and-yellow text-mode interfaces.

PROGRAM CD_Collection;
TYPE CDRecord = RECORD
  Artist, Title: String[40];
  Year, Tracks: Integer;
END;
1998 – 2000 ☕

Object Foundations: Java at University

Studied computer science fundamentals and early Java (JDK 1.1 / Swing applets) at university. Absorbed core object-oriented principles and system design before dropping out two years later to pursue an unconventional, self-directed path in education, language, and clinical communication.

public class LearnerPath {
  // Stepping outside conventional tracks
  boolean autonomous = true;
}
2008 – 2020 🐧

Device Sovereignty: XDA & EasyStroke

Deep dive into open source and hardware sovereignty. Flashing nightly custom ROMs on XDA Developers, running Kubuntu on X11, and obsessing over gesture-based productivity with EasyStroke. That ethos of frictionless gesture control and de-bloated mobile software directly inspired QuickDraw Launcher.

# EasyStroke on Kubuntu X11
stroke_right ➔ launch_app
ethos: 100% open-source & user-sovereign
2024 – 2026 ⚡

The Renaissance: Agentic Vibe Coding

Thirty years later, the friction of syntax has vanished. With agentic AI (Google Antigravity & local Qwen 14B) as pair-programming architects, building ideas into production happens in hours—from QuickDraw Launcher in Kotlin Compose to sovereign Linux dotfiles and custom pedagogy engines.

MANIFESTOS & ESSAYS

Ideas on the Future of Code

Reflections from the cutting edge of AI-human collaboration.

Essay 01

What is Vibe Coding Really?

Vibe coding is not about being sloppy or careless; it is about eliminating friction between the human creative impulse and working software. When you operate with AI agents as an architectural partner, syntax becomes secondary to vision, constraints, and iteration velocity.

Essay 02

The Sovereign Local AI Thesis

Depending 100% on external APIs is a vulnerability. The ultimate developer setup is a hybrid: local open weights (like Qwen 14B on your local GPU or ZimaOS) handling raw crunch and offline operations, with frontier models acting as high-level consultants.

Essay 03

Ethical Web Hosting in the Age of Bloat

Modern websites shouldn't require 40MB of bloated JavaScript or mega-datacenter servers running on coal. By building static, pure-semantic JAMstack sites hosted on renewable-powered providers or your own hardware, the web remains fast, open, and sustainable.

Essay 04

The 30-Year Circle: Turbo Pascal to AI

In 1996, hacking Turbo Pascal on a flickering CRT screen felt like absolute wizardry—you were breathing life into raw silicon byte by byte. Thirty years after building that CD database and exploring early Java, vibe coding with autonomous agents rekindles that exact childlike thrill. The gatekeeping is gone; only your imagination and taste remain.
