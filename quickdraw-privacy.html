Privacy Policy for QuickDraw Launcher

Last Updated: October 9, 2026  |  Developer: IAMGREATER  |  Contact: support@iamgreater.net
Privacy-First Guarantee: QuickDraw Launcher operates 100% offline. It does not contain any analytics libraries, advertising trackers, or background telemetry. No user data, device identifiers, or app usage history is ever collected, transmitted, or sold.

1. Overview & Data Collection

QuickDraw Launcher, developed by IAMGREATER, is designed from the ground up with zero data collection:

  • Zero Data Collected: We do not track which apps you install, open, or add to your grids.
  • No Internet Permission: QuickDraw does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app has no network access and cannot communicate with any remote servers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the launcher.

2. Android Permissions & Usage

QuickDraw requests only the absolute minimum permissions strictly necessary to deliver its overlay and shortcut functionality:

A. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used solely to draw edge touch hitboxes and display your customized app grid when you swipe from screen edges over other applications. No screen content is captured or read.

B. Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Required by Android launcher applications to display your list of installed apps so you can select and organize them into your custom grids. This package list is processed locally and never leaves your device.

C. Accessibility Service (Optional)

If enabled by the user, this permission allows QuickDraw shortcuts to trigger system hardware actions: Lock Screen, Open Notification Shade, Recent Apps, and Take Screenshot. No keystrokes, personal communications, or screen inputs are observed or logged.

3. Data Storage & Backups

All your grid configurations, categories, and settings are saved locally on your device in private app storage. When you use the Backup & Restore feature, configuration files are exported only to locations you explicitly select via the standard Android system file picker.

4. Children's Privacy

QuickDraw Launcher does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
