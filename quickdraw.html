INTERACTION MODEL

How QuickDraw Works

Designed with muscle-memory ergonomics. Learn it in 30 seconds, use it effortlessly forever.

01

One-Time Overlay Toggle

Enable the standard Android "Display over other apps" permission. QuickDraw operates purely as an on-demand screen overlay—no invasive Accessibility Services or background accessibility daemons required.

02

Changeable Trigger Zone (1px–80px)

Position the edge trigger exactly where your thumb naturally rests. Choose right edge, left edge, top, or bottom. Dial thickness down to a razor-thin 1px–10px so full-screen game taps and gestures pass straight through to background apps.

03

Resizeable Grid & Arsenal

Organize your daily apps in an ergonomic 7×7 rectangular grid (or scale up to 9×9). Fine-tune card width and toggle Swipe In, Swipe Up, or Swipe Down gestures to match your personal workflow.

04

Pure Swipe or Second Tap

Pure Swipe Mode: Glide continuously from the screen edge directly onto your app icon and release to launch immediately without lifting your finger.

Second Tap Mode: Swipe to pop open the grid, pause/lift your thumb, then select your app with a clean second tap.

ENGINEERING & PRIVACY

Architected Without Compromise

Built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with the open-source spirit of XDA and Linux EasyStroke.

🔋 Zero Idle Battery Drain

Unlike background sync services, QuickDraw uses an event-driven overlay. When you are not touching the trigger area, the app consumes 0% CPU and zero wake locks.

🛡️ 100% Offline (No Internet Permission)

We deliberately omitted the android.permission.INTERNET permission from the AndroidManifest. The app physically cannot send data to the cloud.

⚡ EasyStroke & XDA Heritage

Inspired by the legendary EasyStroke gesture daemon on Kubuntu Linux and the de-bloated autonomy of XDA custom ROMs. Pure speed, zero ads, zero telemetry.

🎛️ Changeable Trigger Zones (1px–80px)

Fine-tune edge thickness from 1px to 80px, adjust position offset and trigger reach, and configure independent behaviors for vertical (portrait) and horizontal (landscape) orientations.

📱 Target SDK 36 (Android 16 Ready)

Fully compliant with the latest Google Play 2026 requirements, predictive back gestures, edge-to-edge system displays, and high-refresh-rate displays (90Hz / 120Hz).

📐 Resizeable 7×7 Rectangular Grid

Ergonomic default 7×7 compact rectangular layout with a customizable card width slider (65%–98%) and scale support up to 9×9 to suit any device and thumb size.

🎛️ Gesture Toggles & Pure Swipe

Choose between zero-lift Pure Swipe or Swipe + Second Tap. Toggle Swipe In, Swipe Up, and Swipe Down gestures on or off individually in Settings.
