INTERACTION MODEL

How QuickDraw Works

Designed with muscle-memory ergonomics. Learn it in 30 seconds, use it effortlessly forever.

01

One-Time Overlay Toggle

Enable the standard Android "Display over other apps" permission. QuickDraw operates purely as an on-demand screen overlay—no invasive Accessibility Services or background accessibility daemons required.

02

Tune Your Trigger Zone

Position the edge trigger exactly where your thumb naturally rests. Choose the right edge, left edge, or bottom border. Fine-tune touch width, height, and vertical offset.

03

Pin Your Favorite Apps

Choose your daily arsenal—WhatsApp, Camera, Calculator, Browser, or System Settings. Arrange them in customizable rows and columns for effortless muscle-memory access.

04

Glide Inward & Release

From inside ANY app, game, or video, swipe your thumb inward. The floating rectangular grid pops up in 60fps; hover over your target square app tile and release. Seamless context switching in sub-16 milliseconds.

ENGINEERING & PRIVACY

Architected Without Compromise

Built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with the open-source spirit of XDA and Linux EasyStroke.

🔋 Zero Idle Battery Drain

Unlike background sync services, QuickDraw uses an event-driven overlay. When you are not touching the trigger area, the app consumes 0% CPU and zero wake locks.

🛡️ 100% Offline (No Internet Permission)

We deliberately omitted the android.permission.INTERNET permission from the AndroidManifest. The app physically cannot send data to the cloud.

⚡ EasyStroke & XDA Heritage

Inspired by the legendary EasyStroke gesture daemon on Kubuntu Linux and the de-bloated autonomy of XDA custom ROMs. Pure speed, zero ads, zero telemetry.

📱 Target SDK 35 (Android 15)

Fully compliant with the latest Google Play 2026 requirements, predictive back gestures, edge-to-edge system displays, and high-refresh-rate displays (90Hz / 120Hz).

🎮 Game & Video Friendly

Easily reposition or hide the trigger area so it never interferes with games, horizontal video playback, or keyboard typing.
